Brokerage & Property Management Services

AV Realty & Management combines professional real estate brokerage with hands-on property management expertise, giving owners, investors, and clients one trusted partner for every stage of the real estate journey.

Full-Service Property Management

Leasing, maintenance coordination, rent collection, inspections, notices, vendor communication, and resident relations handled with professionalism.

Tenant Placement & Screening

Marketing, showings, applications, credit review, income verification, background checks, and landlord history review to protect your investment.

Rent Collection & Owner Reporting

Structured rent collection, late payment follow-up, monthly owner statements, and direct deposits to keep your property organized and accountable.

Maintenance Coordination

Reliable vendor coordination, emergency response support, repair tracking, and cost-conscious solutions designed to protect the property.

Buyer, Seller & Investor Representation

Strategic representation for clients buying, selling, or investing, with a focus on market value, negotiation, and long-term opportunity.

Investment Strategy & Rental Analysis

Rent projections, market positioning, property evaluation, and practical insight for landlords and investors looking to build income-producing assets.
