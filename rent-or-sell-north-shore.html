When the question comes up

A change in plans can open up a new decision.

You’re moving away

Compare selling before your move with keeping the property under local management. Consider your relocation schedule, preparation needs, and comfort with ownership from a distance.

You inherited a property

Start by organizing condition, occupancy, available records, and immediate property needs. We can discuss brokerage and management options alongside your legal and tax advisors.

You’re tired of managing

Wanting less day-to-day work does not automatically mean you want to sell. Compare exiting ownership with delegating leasing, maintenance coordination, and resident communication.

Common questions

Renting versus selling: questions from owners.

Can you help if I haven’t decided whether to rent or sell?

Yes. That is the purpose of this conversation. We can discuss your situation and identify the property information needed to explore both options.

Can you review both rental potential and sale positioning?

Yes. AV Realty & Management offers both brokerage and property management. We can discuss rental comparisons and relevant sale information, along with the assumptions and limitations of each.

Does my property need to be vacant?

No. Let us know whether you live there, it is tenant-occupied, or some units are vacant. Existing occupancy, agreements, and access arrangements are important parts of planning.

What if I am relocating out of Massachusetts?

We can discuss the practical differences between selling and keeping a North Shore rental under local management, including communication, maintenance coordination, and owner responsibilities.

What if I inherited the property?

We can help assess property condition, occupancy, rental considerations, and sale positioning. Your attorney and tax advisors should address estate authority, ownership, and legal or tax questions.

Can I rent it now and sell it later?

That can be an option, but future plans need to account for lease terms, occupancy, property condition, and applicable obligations. Bring up your likely sale timeline before making leasing decisions.

Will you tell me which option makes the most money?

We can help organize estimates and assumptions for comparison. Neither sale proceeds nor future rental performance can be guaranteed. Your goals, cash needs, risk tolerance, and professional financial or tax advice also matter.

Do I have to hire you after the conversation?

No. An inquiry does not commit you to a listing or management agreement. If you want to proceed, we will discuss the proposed services and terms.
