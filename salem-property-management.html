Questions From Owners

Salem property-management FAQs.

Start with your current situation. We can help identify what information and next steps are needed.

What types of properties do you manage in Salem?

We work with single-family homes, condos, and small multifamily rentals, including occupied properties, vacant units, and homes entering the rental market.

Can you take over if I already have tenants?

Yes. We review available tenancy and operating records, discuss current needs, and coordinate the management transition and resident communication.

Can you help with a partially occupied multifamily?

Yes. We can discuss ongoing management for occupied units alongside preparation and leasing for vacancies, with the work defined in the agreed scope.

Do you work with owners who live outside Massachusetts?

Yes. We can provide a local contact for agreed leasing, resident communication, maintenance coordination, property oversight, and owner reporting.

Can you help with an inherited Salem property?

Yes. We can review occupancy, rents, available records, property condition, and management needs. Estate, probate, tax, and legal matters remain with the appropriate professionals.

How are repairs approved and funded?

The management agreement establishes the coordination scope, owner approval arrangements, and funding procedures before management begins.

How much does management cost?

Request a written proposal for your property. We will explain management fees, applicable leasing or renewal charges, and other agreed costs before you proceed.

How do I get started?

Use the property-review form or call (978) 277-3435. We’ll discuss your property and outline the next steps.
