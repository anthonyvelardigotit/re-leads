Owner Questions

North Shore Property Management FAQ

What North Shore areas do you manage?

Our featured local property-management markets are Swampscott, Marblehead, Salem, and Nahant. We also consider properties throughout surrounding North Shore communities based on location, property type, size, condition, and service needs.

How much does property management cost?

Pricing depends on the property, number of units, level of service, and whether leasing or tenant placement is needed. AV Realty & Management reviews fees clearly before a management agreement is signed.

Can you take over management when a tenant is already living in the property?

Yes. Management can begin with an existing resident in place. The transition generally includes reviewing available lease and property records, establishing communication, setting up payment procedures, and clarifying how maintenance and future requests will be handled.

Do you manage properties for owners who live out of state?

Yes. Local management can be especially useful for owners who are not nearby and need someone to coordinate resident communication, maintenance, inspections, and property matters on the ground.

Do you work with housing voucher tenancies?

AV Realty & Management can assist with administrative coordination related to voucher tenancies, inspections, documentation, and housing-authority communication when applicable to the property and tenancy.

Do you offer leasing or tenant placement without full management?

Leasing and tenant-placement needs can be discussed separately from ongoing management. The appropriate structure depends on the property and the level of support the owner wants.
