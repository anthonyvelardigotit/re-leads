Financing & Expense Details

Purchase analysis using a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan. No appreciation, rent growth, tax benefits, sale proceeds, or refinance proceeds are modeled. Mortgage insurance and other loan charges are not added automatically. If applicable, account for them separately before relying on the cash-flow estimate.

DSCR here is NOI divided by mortgage P&I. Lenders may use different income, reserve, insurance, and debt-service definitions. This is a planning tool, not underwriting or an investment recommendation.

Understand the Results

Know what each number includes.

NOI

Collected rent plus other income, less operating expenses. Excludes debt service, capital reserve, and income taxes in this model.

Cap Rate

Annual NOI divided by purchase price. Initial repairs and closing costs are not included in this denominator.

Cash-on-Cash Return

Annual cash flow after capital reserve and mortgage P&I divided by down payment, closing costs, initial improvements, and initial cash reserve.

Debt Coverage

This tool’s DSCR compares NOI to mortgage P&I. It displays N/A when there is no mortgage debt service.

Capital Reserve

An assumed amount set aside for larger replacements. Cash flow is reduced by this allowance even when the money has not yet been spent.

Cash Flow

The modeled amount remaining after operating expenses, capital reserve, and mortgage P&I. It is not accounting profit or a total investment return.
