Questions From Owners

Marblehead property-management FAQs.

Your property’s needs shape the management plan. Start with a conversation about its current condition, occupancy, and your goals.

What kinds of properties do you manage?

We work with Marblehead single-family homes, condos, and small multifamily rentals, including occupied properties, vacant units, and homes transitioning to rental use.

Can you take over an occupied rental?

Yes. We review available leases, rent records, property information, and current needs before coordinating the transition and resident communication.

Can you help if I live outside Massachusetts?

Yes. We can provide a local point of contact for the agreed leasing, communication, maintenance coordination, oversight, and reporting responsibilities.

Can you help with an inherited property?

Yes. We can help review occupancy, rents, property condition, vacancies, and management needs. Estate, probate, tax, and legal matters remain with the appropriate professionals.

What if I am moving away and want to rent my home?

We can discuss rental positioning, property preparation, leasing, tenant placement, and management after move-in. For a condo, we can also review available association information relevant to the rental plan.

How are maintenance costs and approvals handled?

The management agreement establishes the scope of coordination, owner funding procedures, and approval arrangements. We discuss these before management begins.

How much does management cost?

Request a written proposal for your property. We will explain the management fee, applicable leasing or renewal charges, and any other agreed costs before you proceed.

How do I get started?

Use the property-review form or call (978) 277-3435. We’ll discuss your situation and outline the next steps.
