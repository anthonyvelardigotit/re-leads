Questions From Owners

Marblehead Property Management FAQ

What types of rental properties do you manage in Marblehead?

We work with single-family rentals, condos, and small multifamily properties. That includes occupied rentals, vacant properties preparing for lease-up, and homes transitioning from owner occupancy to rental use.

Can you take over management if I already have a tenant?

Yes. We can review the existing rental setup and available documents, establish management procedures, communicate the transition, and take over the agreed day-to-day responsibilities.

Do you help Marblehead owners who live outside Massachusetts?

Yes. Remote ownership is one of the situations where local management can be especially useful. We can provide a local point of contact for leasing, resident communication, maintenance coordination, property oversight, and owner reporting.

Can you help if I inherited a rental property in Marblehead?

Yes. We can help with the real-estate and property-management side of the property, including occupancy, rents, condition, vacancies, repairs, leasing potential, and ongoing management. Estate, probate, tax, and legal matters should be handled with the appropriate professionals.

Can you manage my Marblehead home if I am moving away?

Yes. We can help evaluate rental positioning, coordinate make-ready work, market the property, screen applicants, complete the leasing process, and provide ongoing management after move-in.

How much does Marblehead property management cost?

Pricing depends on the property, unit count, occupancy, and services needed. After a quick review, we can provide a written proposal that clearly explains the management arrangement and any applicable leasing fees.
