Inherited Property Support

Inherited a North Shore rental? Start with the facts.

Before deciding whether to keep, rent, improve, or sell, it helps to understand the property’s current rental and operational picture.

Turn unfamiliar responsibilities into an organized starting point.

We can help review the property management and real estate side of the transition while you work with your attorney, accountant, or estate representative on legal authority, probate, and tax matters.

  • Who currently occupies the property?
  • What rent is being collected?
  • What leases and tenancy records are available?
  • What is the condition of the units and common areas?
  • Which maintenance needs require attention first?
  • Are any units vacant or approaching turnover?
  • What rental income might the property support?
  • How does ownership fit your long-term goals?

Common Questions

A little clarity before you begin.

Have a question about your specific property? We’re happy to discuss it.

Can you manage my property if I live outside Massachusetts?

Yes. We provide local North Shore management for owners who live out of state, travel frequently, or prefer to delegate day-to-day property responsibilities.

Can you take over a property that already has tenants?

Yes. We begin by reviewing existing occupancy, rent, available leases and records, property condition, and current operating needs to plan the transition.

What if I inherited a property with incomplete records?

We can help organize the available tenancy and operating information and identify gaps. Estate authority, probate, tax, and legal questions remain with your attorney, accountant, or estate representative.

Can you coordinate repairs while I am out of state?

Yes. Maintenance and vendor coordination can be included in management. The scope, owner approvals, and funding procedures are established as part of the management relationship.

Can you prepare a vacant inherited unit for rent?

Depending on the property’s needs and agreed scope, we can coordinate cleaning, repairs, vendor access, rental pricing, marketing, and tenant placement.

Can you help estimate rental value?

Yes. Request a free rent analysis for property-specific rental pricing guidance.

What if I am considering selling instead?

AV Realty & Management is also a real estate brokerage. We can discuss rental operations and brokerage services so you can consider the options that fit your goals.

Do I need to be physically present?

Many management responsibilities can be coordinated remotely. We will discuss property access, required authorizations, available records, and decisions that need your involvement during onboarding.
