Frequently Asked Questions

Remote & Inherited Property Management FAQs

Can you manage my property if I live outside Massachusetts?

Yes. This service is suited to owners who need reliable local property management because they live outside Massachusetts, travel frequently, or simply do not want to handle day-to-day management themselves.

Can you take over a property that already has tenants?

Yes. We can begin by understanding existing occupancy, rent, available documentation, property condition and current operational needs.

What if I inherited the property and don't know much about the tenants or agreements?

An organized initial review can help establish the property management picture while you coordinate estate, probate, tax or legal questions with the appropriate professionals.

Can you coordinate repairs if I live out of state?

Maintenance and vendor coordination are core management functions. The specific scope, approvals and owner funding procedures can be established as part of the management relationship.

Can you help prepare a vacant inherited unit for rent?

Yes. Depending on the property's needs, we can coordinate cleaning, repair work, vendor access, rental pricing, marketing and tenant placement.

Can you estimate what the property should rent for?

Yes. Request a free rent analysis for property-specific rental pricing guidance.

What if I inherited the property but may want to sell it?

AV Realty & Management is also a real estate brokerage, so we can discuss real estate services if selling better fits your goals.

Do I need to be physically present for you to manage the property?

Remote ownership is one of the main reasons owners hire professional management. We can discuss what can be handled locally and what authorizations or owner decisions are needed.
