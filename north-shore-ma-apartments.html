Rental Search FAQ

North Shore Rental Questions

A few common questions about using this page to organize your apartment search.

What areas of the North Shore can I search?

You can submit preferences for Salem, Swampscott, Beverly, Lynn, Marblehead, Nahant, Peabody, Danvers, Revere, and surrounding Massachusetts communities. If you are flexible, list multiple locations in your form.

Does submitting this form guarantee that I will get an apartment?

No. The form is a rental-search intake tool. Availability changes frequently, and each landlord or property may have different application, screening, affordability, occupancy, and approval requirements.

Why do you ask for an approximate household income range?

The income field is optional and provides context for your search. You may leave it blank and discuss affordability questions directly with us. Property-specific application requirements are reviewed separately.

Can I submit the form if I am flexible on location?

Yes. In fact, flexibility can make it easier to identify potential options. You can list several communities or simply state that you are open to multiple North Shore areas.

Can I search for pet-friendly apartments?

Yes. Use the pets field and notes section to describe your pet situation. Pet policies, restrictions, deposits, fees, and other requirements are determined by each property or housing provider.

Can I also browse listings myself?

Yes. Use the live listings link on this page to browse available properties through the search portal, then contact AV Realty & Management if you would like help with a specific opportunity.
