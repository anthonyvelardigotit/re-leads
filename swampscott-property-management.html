Questions From Owners

Swampscott Property Management FAQ

What types of rental properties do you manage in Swampscott?

We work with single-family rentals, condos, and small multifamily properties. That includes occupied properties, vacancies, owner-occupied multifamily properties transitioning to rentals, and properties that need make-ready coordination before leasing.

Can you take over management if I already have a tenant?

Yes. We can review the existing rental setup and available documents, establish new management procedures, communicate the transition, and take over the agreed day-to-day responsibilities.

Do you help owners who live outside Massachusetts?

Yes. Remote ownership is one of the situations where a local property manager can add the most value. We can serve as the local point of contact for leasing, tenant communication, maintenance coordination, property oversight, and reporting.

Can you help with an inherited property?

Yes. We can help with the real-estate and property-management side of the property, including understanding occupancy, rents, condition, vacancies, repairs, leasing potential, and ongoing management. Estate, probate, tax, and legal questions should be handled with the appropriate professionals.

How much does property management cost?

Pricing depends on the property, unit count, occupancy, and services needed. After a quick review, we can provide a written proposal that clearly explains management and any applicable leasing fees.
