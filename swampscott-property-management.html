Attention to the Actual Property

A practical plan for the building you own.

A coastal home, a condo, and an owner-occupied multifamily can require different operating plans. We help organize the details before responsibilities are handed over.

  • Condition and maintenance priorities Review available information about building systems, exterior upkeep, and immediate repair concerns.
  • Occupancy and tenancy records Organize available leases, rent information, renewal dates, and the current rental setup.
  • Shared spaces and responsibilities Clarify parking, storage, common areas, utilities, landscaping, snow removal, and vendor access.
  • Vacancy preparation Identify cleaning, repairs, pricing, and marketing needs before a unit returns to the rental market.

Questions From Owners

Swampscott property-management FAQs.

Tell us where you are starting. We can help identify the next practical step.

Are you based in Swampscott?

Yes. AV Realty & Management is based at 5 Huron Street in Swampscott and serves rental-property owners across nearby North Shore communities.

What kinds of properties do you manage?

We work with single-family homes, condos, and small multifamily rentals, including occupied properties, vacant units, and homes entering the rental market.

Can you help with an owner-occupied multifamily?

Yes. We can discuss management responsibilities for the rental units and clarify shared spaces, access, and the responsibilities you want to retain.

Can you take over if tenants are already in place?

Yes. We review available tenancy and operating records and plan resident communication and the transition around the current occupancy.

Do you offer leasing without ongoing management?

We can discuss tenant-placement assistance separately from ongoing management. The proposed scope and applicable fees are outlined before you proceed.

Can you help if I live outside Massachusetts?

Yes. We can act as a local contact for the agreed leasing, communication, maintenance coordination, reporting, and oversight responsibilities.

How much does management cost?

Request a written proposal for your property. We will explain management fees, applicable leasing or renewal charges, and other agreed costs before you proceed.

How do I get started?

Use the property-review form or call (978) 277-3435. We’ll discuss your situation and outline the next steps.
