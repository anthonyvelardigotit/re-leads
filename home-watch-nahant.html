A visit with a purpose

What we look for during a home check.

A practical walkthrough, followed by an update so you know what we observed.

01 / HEAT

Temperature & heating

Check the thermostat reading and look for obvious signs that the heat is operating as expected.

02 / WATER

Visible leaks & moisture

Look for visible leaks, unusual moisture, and water staining in accessible areas, including around sinks and exposed plumbing.

03 / ACCESS

Doors & windows

Check accessible doors and windows for obvious damage, unexpected openings, or signs of forced entry.

04 / EXTERIOR

Property condition

Walk accessible exterior areas and look for visible damage, fallen branches, or other changes that need your attention.

05 / COMMUNICATION

A clear owner update

Receive a brief update after each visit, with photos of any concerns observed and a discussion of next steps.

06 / FOLLOW-THROUGH

Help arranging next steps

If an issue comes up, we can discuss arranging a contractor or an additional visit, with your approval and separately agreed charges.

Checks cover visible, accessible conditions at the time of the visit. Access, weather conditions, and your agreed service checklist determine each walkthrough’s scope.

Before you book

Home watch, explained.

A few questions homeowners ask when planning time away.

What is home watch?

Home watch means scheduled visits to an unoccupied home to look for visible issues and keep the owner informed. Our visits focus on the checklist and access arrangements agreed with you before service starts.

Can you check my Nahant home while I’m away for the winter?

Yes. We offer seasonal home watch arrangements in Nahant and Swampscott, including for owners planning several months away. Contact us with your dates so we can confirm availability and discuss your home.

How often will you visit?

Weekly visits are an option. We’ll agree on a schedule based on your home, your travel plans, and the checks you want completed. Additional visits can be discussed separately.

How much does home watch cost?

Pricing depends on the property’s size, location, visit frequency, and the scope of the checks. Send your address and travel dates for a custom quote with a clear fee before service begins.

What happens if you notice a problem?

We contact you with the observations and available photos. If you would like help arranging a contractor or a follow-up visit, we’ll discuss authorization and any additional charges with you.

Can you help while I decide whether to rent or sell?

We can discuss home watch while the property is unoccupied, along with AV Realty’s tenant placement, property management, or sales services if your plans change.
