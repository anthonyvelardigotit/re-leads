Discuss Selling Your Property

A closer look at ownership

Different properties need different questions.

A single-family home, condo, and multifamily building each come with a different set of documents, costs, and maintenance considerations.

Homes & coastal properties

Consider layout, major systems, exterior condition, drainage, and maintenance needs. For coastal properties, include property-specific exposure and insurance questions in your review.

Condominiums

Look beyond the unit to association fees, available financial records, reserves, planned projects, rules, and any applicable rental restrictions.

Multifamily buildings

Review available leases, rent records, occupancy, utility responsibilities, common areas, and maintenance needs alongside the purchase price.

Swampscott investment property

Understand the operation behind the asking price.

For an investor or owner-occupant buyer, the acquisition is only part of the decision. The property also needs a workable plan for leasing, maintenance, expenses, and ongoing ownership.

Our brokerage and management services bring those considerations into the same conversation, helping you identify assumptions to verify before moving forward.

Are you based in Swampscott?

Can you help with both buying and selling?

Yes. We offer buyer and seller services for homes, condos, and multifamily properties. If you are coordinating a sale and a purchase, we can discuss how the timelines fit together.

Do you work with first-time buyers and relocation buyers?

Yes. We can help explain the process, focus your search, coordinate showings, and keep questions and transaction steps organized with your lender and other professionals.
