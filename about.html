Meet the Founder

A Practical Approach to Real Estate

Anthony Velardi is a licensed real estate professional, property manager, and founder of AV Realty & Management. His experience includes hands-on multifamily property management, residential operations, leasing, client service, and working with real estate investors.

Through that work, Anthony saw how often real estate clients are forced to navigate fragmented advice — one person for a transaction, another for leasing, another for management, and another perspective entirely when evaluating an investment. AV Realty & Management was created to offer a more connected approach.

The firm's philosophy is straightforward: understand the property, understand the client's goal, communicate clearly, and follow through. Whether the assignment involves a rental property, a home purchase, a sale, tenant placement, or ongoing management, the objective is to make decisions with both the immediate need and the long-term outcome in mind.

Anthony's perspective is also shaped by an appreciation for the role housing plays in people's lives. That experience reinforces a commitment to treating owners, residents, buyers, sellers, and investors with professionalism and respect while keeping the financial realities of real estate front and center.

Real estate is ultimately about responsibility. Clients are trusting you with homes, investments, income, and major life decisions. My goal is to give every client thoughtful guidance, clear communication, and the confidence that their interests are being taken seriously.

How We Work

What AV Realty & Management Stands For

The company was built around a few simple principles that apply whether we are managing a rental, advising an owner, helping a buyer, or representing a real estate client.

1

Clear Communication

Clients should understand what is happening, what comes next, and why a recommendation is being made. Straightforward communication is part of the service.

2

Operational Discipline

Good management requires follow-through. Leasing, maintenance, resident communication, documentation, and owner reporting all depend on consistent execution.

3

Long-Term Thinking

Real estate decisions should be evaluated beyond the immediate transaction. Cash flow, property condition, market positioning, risk, and future value all matter.

4

Client Advocacy

Every client has different priorities. The job is to understand those priorities and provide representation that is thoughtful, responsive, and aligned with the client's goals.

5

Local Perspective

North Shore communities each have their own rental markets, property types, demand patterns, and neighborhood dynamics. Local context matters when making real estate decisions.

6

Respect for the Asset

A property is often one of a client's most valuable assets. Management and brokerage decisions should reflect that responsibility and protect long-term value wherever possible.
